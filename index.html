Chhath Ghat

Patna · on the banks of Ganga ji

Join devotees on the ghats of Patna. At the Chhath Bazaar on either end of the ghat, pick up a soop & daura and fill it with thekua 🍪, kela 🍌, nariyal 🥥, ganna 🎋 & santra 🍊 from the stalls, then wade into the Ganga and offer Arghya to Surya Dev at sunset (Sandhya) and sunrise (Usha). Float diyas on the river with everyone who's online.
